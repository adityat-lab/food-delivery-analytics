# Key Insights

- Average delivery time is **26.3 min** (median 26, 90th percentile 40).
- Deliveries in **Jam** traffic take 31.2 min vs 21.3 min in Low traffic (**+46%**).
- **Fog** weather is the slowest (29.0 min); **Sunny** is the fastest (21.9 min).
- Peak ordering hours are **19:00, 20:00, 21:00**.
- Distance and delivery time have a correlation of **0.32**.
- Rider rating vs delivery time correlation: **-0.36** (negative = higher-rated riders deliver faster).
- Festival days add about **19.5 min** to the average delivery.
- Carrying multiple orders raises time from 22.9 min (0) to 47.9 min (3).
- Fastest vehicle type: **Electric_Scooter** (24.4 min avg).
